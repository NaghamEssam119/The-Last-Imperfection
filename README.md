## 2. Project Idea

**Logline:** In a world of identical, flawless dolls, Loza—a handmade doll with mismatched button eyes, visible stitches, and patched clothes—visits a restoration center to become perfect. Every repaired imperfection erases a precious memory of the child who loved and made her. Before losing the final patch over her heart, Loza chooses to reclaim her original appearance and memories, inspiring other dolls to embrace their uniqueness.

**Genre:** Emotional drama / Fantasy / Animated short film  
**Visual Style:** AI-generated cinematic stop-motion, handmade puppet aesthetics, fabric and wood textures, miniature environments, expressive lighting and camera work.  
**Themes:** Identity, memory, self-acceptance, imperfection, love and individuality.  
**Core Message:** Imperfections can preserve the stories that shape us; perfection does not necessarily mean beauty.  
**Target Duration:** 7 minutes (within DEPI requirement of 4–9 minutes)  
**Narrative Structure:** 14 scenes, approximately 30 seconds per scene.  
**Narration:** Loza tells the story herself in Egyptian Arabic, using an emotional voiceover from her own perspective.  
**Primary AI Video Tool:** Google Flow.

### Narrative Arc
1. **Setup:** Loza feels out of place among identical, flawless dolls.
2. **Conflict:** A restoration process removes her imperfections and also erases her memories.
3. **Climax:** The final patch over her heart reveals the memory of the child who loved her.
4. **Resolution:** Loza stops the process, recovers her patches and memories, and inspires others to celebrate their individuality.

## 4. Work Plan

The team follows a **flexible, iterative workflow** rather than fixed internal deadlines. Completed work is discussed and reviewed as it becomes available. The team uploads progress to a shared Google Drive folder at the end of each week.

| Phase | Activities | Owner(s) | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Pre-production | Develop film concept, design Loza's character, write Arabic script, voiceover script, and 14-scene plan | Nagham | Character reference, scripts and scene plan |
| 2 — Prompt engineering | Translate the scene plan into consistent AI visual/video prompts | Mostafa | Scene prompts and visual instructions |
| 3 — Audio production | Prepare and produce Loza's voiceover according to the approved script | Abdelaziz | Voiceover audio assets |
| 4 — AI scene production | Generate shots/scenes using character references and prompts in Google Flow | Abdelaziz, Samar, Rana, Manar | AI-generated footage for scenes 1–14 |
| 5 — Collaborative review | Review the generated footage, character consistency and storytelling; request revisions when necessary | All team members, coordinated by Nagham | Reviewed scene versions |
| 6 — Post-production | Assemble and edit scenes, trim footage, integrate voiceover, add transitions and create the outro | Nagham | Edited cinematic film |
| 7 — Final quality review | Verify pacing, visuals, sound, continuity and duration | Nagham and team | Final approved export |
| 8 — Presentation & delivery | Prepare a presentation covering concept, process, AI tools and creative decisions; organize deliverables | Mostafa (presentation) with support from the team; Nagham coordinates delivery | Presentation, film and supporting files |

### Weekly Collaboration Cycle

1. Each member develops their assigned work at their own pace.
2. Newly completed work is shared with the team for discussion and feedback.
3. Required changes are incorporated before approval.
4. Completed deliverables and progress are uploaded to the shared **Google Drive** folder at the end of each week.
5. The Team Leader tracks outstanding work and ensures consistency across the film.

- **Story continuity:** The restoration machine temporarily stores removed pieces and their associated memories, allowing Loza's recovery near the ending.
- **Timing note:** The outro will be included in the planned runtime where possible so that the final cut remains within the required duration.
